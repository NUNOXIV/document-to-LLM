## The Foundation for Modern Cyber Defense

The CIS Critical Security Controls® (CIS Controls®) is the foundation organizations need to defend against today's most common and impactful cyber threats.

## Your Path to Defensible Security

Follow the five milestones to build stronger defenses and maintain informed, resilient operations.

## Implement

CIS Controls mitigate over 90% of the most common cyberattacks.

## Measure

Identify what's working and where to improve. Use measurable data and assessments to evaluate the effectiveness of your controls and maturity over time.

## What's Next?

The CIS Controls provide advanced, intelligent, and proactive security operations.

With this strong core security in place, enterprises can confidently and securely manage advanced capabilities such as:

-   User and entity behavior analytics (UEBA) for anomaly detection
-   CI/CD pipeline security to protect modern development workflows
-   Threat hunting, threat intelligence, and attack simulations powered by consistent, accurate control data

## CIS Controls Tools

CIS SecureSuite® Platform, Controls Module (formerly CIS CSAT Pro)  CONTROLS SELF ASSESSMENT TOOL Helps teams track and document their progress in implementing the CIS Controls. Progress can be compared to industry averages.

## CIS Risk Assessment Method (CIS RAM)

A method and tool to let enterprises of varying security capabilities navigate the balance between implementing security controls, risks, and organizational needs.

## CIS Controls Assessment Specification

Identifies specific tests for Safeguards that can be automated, and provides a specification for vendors to implement them.

## CIS Controls Navigator

Online tool to compare CIS Safeguards with recommendations found in other security frameworks.

## CIS BenchWork

CIS collaboration platform for volunteers and CIS staff to share ideas, develop content, and learn from each other.

## CIS Community Defense Model (CIS CDM)

Identifies the security value of CIS Safeguards against specific attacks identified by open data sources, and described using the MITRE ATT&amp;CK Framework.

## Navigating an Ocean of Cyber Frameworks

Authoritative and vetted cross-mappings from our security best practices applied to well-known target frameworks or standards. For current versions and information on mappings, go to www.cisecurity.org/controls/v8-1
